# DESIGN.md — Waqas Sharif portfolio

## What this is
A design system for a personal engineering portfolio website. It is not a
brand kit and it carries no fixed tokens yet. It states the constraints and
the intent. You choose the specific values and tell me why.

## Audience
Hiring engineers and engineering managers assessing a candidate for agentic
AI and LLM reliability work. Masters admissions readers second. Technical,
skimming first and reading deeply second, and distrustful of anything that
looks marketed at them.

## Goal
Make dense, evidence-heavy content readable. Long prose, dated entries,
numbers with sources, collapsible detail, and a vertical timeline with
items on both sides of a centre spine. This is a document that should look
considered, not a landing page.

## Direction
Editorial and typographic. Closer to a well-set technical report or a
scientific journal than to a SaaS marketing page.

One restrained accent colour, used only to mark what is verifiable: links
to repositories, live applications, credentials. Nothing else takes the
accent.

Generous line height and a comfortable measure. Text is the primary
element, not a supporting one.

Flat. No gradients anywhere. No glassmorphism. No drop shadows used to fake
depth.

## Hard constraints
Not Inter and not Roboto.
Legible at 360px wide as well as on a desktop.
Light and dark must both hold.
Every visual distinction must survive with colour removed. Nothing may rely
on colour alone.
Collapsed and expanded states must both be legible, because long content is
disclosed rather than hidden.

## What to leave to me
Exact colour values, the typeface, the scale and the spacing rhythm are
yours to choose. Choose deliberately and state the reasoning for the type
and the accent colour before I give feedback.
